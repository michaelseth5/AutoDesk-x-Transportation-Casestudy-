Creating a Higher-Performing Mobility Ecosystem

🚆

Real-Rail-First

Builds on the C Line, K Line, and Metro's selected Sepulveda alternative — not a new corridor from scratch.

🔄

Transfer Synergy

Every rail-to-bus junction is held to a 10-minute max transfer window, with a buffer if the train runs late.

📍

Station-Area Density

A 0.5 mi walkshed mapped around every stop informs where infill and local business investment pays off first.

🚗

Three-Tier Last-Mile

Rail, scheduled bus feeders, and synced rideshare pickup give every rider a real option for the final mile.

🚦

Peak-Hour Priority

Dedicated bus lanes and transit signal priority keep feeder convoys moving during the 6–9am / 4–7pm peaks.

🔥

Olympic-Ready

LA28 venues are mapped against the real network today, so venue-day demand has a plan before 2028.
